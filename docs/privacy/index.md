# Mini Vault privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-15

Mini Vault is a hobby app for tracking a tabletop miniature collection. This page says what it stores about you, where,
and how to get rid of it.

## What Mini Vault stores

- **Your account**: your email address and your password. The password is handled by the sign-in server and stored only
  as a hash, never in plain text.
- **Your Collections and everything in them**: Units, Categories, Workflows and Stages, the history of Stage Changes,
  notes, Tags, Paints, Paint Schemes and anything else you type into the app.
- **Unit photos** you take or pick in the app.

Mini Vault does not ask for your name, location, contacts or anything else.

## Where it is stored

- **On your device**: the app keeps a local database so it works offline.
- **On the Mini Vault server**: a server run by the developer for this app, which syncs your data between your devices.
  Only your account can read your data.

Your data is not shared with, sold to or shown to anyone else. Mini Vault has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no
crash reporting, and no third-party service receives your data.

## Deleting your data

- **Everything**: in the app, open the account menu and choose **Delete account**. This deletes, on the server, your
  account, every Collection and everything in it, and all your photos, then signs you out, which clears this device.
  It cannot be undone.
- **Only this device**: sign out or uninstall the app. This removes the local copy; your data stays on the server until
  you delete your account.

## How long it is kept

Your data is kept until you delete it or delete your account. Anything you delete in the app is deleted from the server
too, and deleting your account takes effect at once.

## Children

Mini Vault is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child
has an account, ask for it to be deleted (see Contact).

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new date. Every past version is in the
[project's repository](https://github.com/ItsMajorSpace/mini-vault/commits/main/docs/privacy/index.md).
