Workflow Mockups

Workflows list, Stage editor, Stage sheet, Delete Stage, Switch Workflow and the Collection dialogs. Grimdark theme throughout, one Fantasy frame. Sample: the starter Workflow "Standard" (Wishlist → Done, Owned from Unassembled, Finished from Done) used by Ultramarines and Death Guard. No domain changes: Stages stay linear, exactly one Owned and one Finished Stage, Finished at or after Owned.

  • One ReorderableListView holding Stages and two ZoneMarker items, all with explicit ReorderableDragStartListener handles. Stages between markers form a zone card (rounded first and last row, left rail: dashed outline = Wanted, half accent = Owned, accent = Finished).
